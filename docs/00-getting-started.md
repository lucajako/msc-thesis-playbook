# Getting started

If you would like to start your thesis with us, you have to first contact one of the professors of our [research group](https://www.bonsai.deib.polimi.it/people/) to enquire about current thesis in their research area.

I am doing my PhD in IT under the supervision of Prof. Giacomo Verticale. In his team, we are currently working on network security (attack detection accelerated with eBPF), applied cryptography (zero knowledge middleboxes), and quantum key distribution (mathematical modelling).

If you feel you are interested in one of these topics, please do not hesitate to contact us!
