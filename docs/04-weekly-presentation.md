# Meeting presentation

Your presentation should be concise and to the point. It should composed of less than 10 slides if you do not have extensive results or some comparison between the previous results and the current ones to show.

The following structure is a draft for your weekly presentaton:

* Slide 1 — Goal of the week
  * What question were you trying to answer?

* Slide 2 — What changed since last meeting
  * Implementation / reading / experiments.

* Slide 3 — Methodology
  * Experimental setup or technical approach.

* Slide 4–6 — Results
  * Graphs, tables, observations.

* Slide 7 — Interpretation
  * What do the results mean?

* Slide 8 — Next steps / Questions
  * What will you do next?
  * What decisions or help do you need?
